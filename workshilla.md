**Introducing Workshilla — Open-Source Work \& Team Progress Tracking**



Project managers and team leads often spend too much time chasing updates, checking task status, and trying to understand where a project really stands.



That’s where stands **Workshilla** — a lightweight, open-source platform designed to bring **projects, daily updates, team allocation, progress tracking, and project health** into one place.



**What can Workshilla help you with?**



✅ **Centralized project tracking** — Keep project information, timelines, teams, and progress together.

✅ **Daily work updates** — Capture hours, work summaries, blockers, and status updates.

✅ **Team visibility** — Understand who is working on what and where attention is required.

✅ **Project health at a glance** — Quickly identify projects that are on track, delayed, or blocked.

✅ Role-based access — Give managers, project managers, leads, and developers the visibility relevant to their role.

✅ **Team management** — Manage project members, assignments, and team allocation.

✅ **Audit \& history** — Maintain continuity of past work and management activities.

✅ **Open Source** — Transparent, customizable, and available for teams to extend.



**One of the key advantages**: On-Premise Deployment



Workshilla can be installed within your own team/server environment, giving organizations greater control over their data and deployment environment.



No need to depend entirely on an external SaaS platform — run it where your team needs it.
